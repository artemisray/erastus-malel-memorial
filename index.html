The Journey of a Life

His Story

01

Early Life

Erastus was born on 09 November 1978 at Kaptoboiti. He was the last-born child of the late George Soimo and Agnes Soimo. He was a brother to Eddah, Fredrick, Josphine, Japhet, Alfred, Emily, Zeddy and Raymond, and an uncle to many.

02

Education & Work

He attended Kaptoboiti Primary School from 1985 to 1994, then Marinyin Secondary School from 1995 to 1998, before venturing into business.

03

Family

Erastus married Clara Malel in 2008. Together they were blessed with two children, Sheen Kipruto and Rihanna Chepngeno.

04

Faith & Remembrance

During his childhood, Erastus was a member of Kaptoboiti AGC Church. He is remembered with love by his family, friends and community.

05

Health and Demise

Erastus enjoyed good health until August 2026, when he started complaining of severe pain in his leg. He sought treatment on and off at various hospitals, including Kericho Referral Hospital and Medistar Hospital, where he finally received his last treatment. He rested on 23 October 2026. Though his journey on earth came to an end, his memory will forever remain in our hearts.
